Visit protocol: responsible community-based tourism

Photos only with permission

Some people, places and moments are not photographed.

The Intangible Zone is off-limits

No route enters the territory of peoples in voluntary isolation; at any sign of their presence, the group turns back.

Knowledge belongs to the community

Medicinal plants, songs and stories are not recorded without consent.

No gifts or cash handouts

Your contribution goes to the community fund.

Safety and access FAQ

Is it safe to travel to the Ecuadorian Amazon?

The U.S. keeps Ecuador at Level 2 (advisory of August 18, 2026); Pastaza and Orellana are not at Levels 3 or 4. We check the advisory before every departure and reschedule at no cost if it changes.

How do I get to Waorani territory?

Quito → Shell by road (about 5 hours) → light aircraft to the Shiripuno River (about 35 minutes, included) → canoe to the community.

Which vaccines do I need?

See a travel doctor. Yellow fever vaccination is recommended for the Ecuadorian Amazon.
